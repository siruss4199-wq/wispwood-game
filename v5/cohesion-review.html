Two focused gameplay changes

Supply Surge now raises every non-rare pull by one tier above its normal result, including decoded sources. Spend the shared three pulls on whichever family needs help. Guaranteed rare pulls keep their schedule and do not consume Surge.

During final preparation, a one-time optional Vault trade competes for a real vault ingredient. It returns equipment for the other required family, 8 energy and three rich pulls. Keeping the ingredient finishes sooner; trading redirects resources. There is no new currency, timer or mandatory step.

Seven built-in image edits, no retries. Runtime sprites are 192px transparent WebP; new files total about 100KB. Remaining higher-tier SVGs and other container types still need future art work. Browser emulation is not physical iPhone testing.
