How to play

  1. Tap a producer (Rivet Rover first) to make parts. Each tap costs 1 energy.
  2. Merge two matching parts by tapping one, then the other, or by dragging one onto the other.
  3. Deliver the parts the coast task asks for. Each task restores a place on the coast.
  4. Crew orders take spare parts and pay energy. At zero energy, a field meal is always free.
