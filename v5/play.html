    Coast Camp

    A new way to play Vaultwake: crew orders earn coins, coins upgrade the coast.

    Play Classic VaultwakeSound and vibration check

    How to play

    1. Tap a generator for a part (1 ϟ). Tap a crate twice for a free part.
    2. Drag a part onto its twin to merge them. Merging, opening cargo and moving are all done by dragging; a tap only shows what a thing is.
    3. Deliver crew orders for coins and +1 ϟ. Orders ask for better parts as the beach is built and your generators grow; the gold order brings the materials for the next stage of a place and pays half as much again.
    4. Stuck on an order? Tap it, then New order: three changes a day are free, more after a short ad. The gold order is the coast's next stage and stays.
    5. Go to the coast (tap the strip under the orders) and build there: a stage needs its materials (its gold order delivered) and its coins. Each place is upgraded in three stages, and each stage changes the beach and tells a bit of the story. When the beach's last stage is done, the Survey Worksite opens up the cliff: seven more places, and half of its crews want Rope Winch parts.
    6. Sealed cargo surrounds Rivet Rover's camp in the middle of the board. Each crate shows the well-merged part that opens it (deeper crates want higher parts): lay that part on it. Inside: parts, crates, coins, a little energy and Tide tokens. The more useful the find, the harder the part it asks for; tokens glow green. A Tide token laid on a spare part turns it into the part of the same level (Lv) and another kind that an order still needs; while you hold or drag it, the parts it works on glow. If no order is missing such a part, keep the token for later.
    7. Tideglass half-buries parts around the camp. It takes no part: a merge beside it cracks it if what it makes is at least as strong as the part inside; the third crack breaks it and the part is yours.
    8. The edge of the board was packed by other crews: its crates want Survey Kite and Rope Winch parts, so it opens as those generators arrive.
    9. Level up a generator to find the next one: at level 3, Rivet Rover brings out the Survey Kite; the Kite brings out the Rope Winch, then the Shell Trap and the Lantern Kiln. Generators grow by merging: drag a generator onto another of the same kind and level (Rivet Rover Lv1 + Lv1 → Lv2). Higher levels make better parts. Copies come from upgrading the beach (the boardwalk, the Workshop, the signal flags and later stages). Each new player level gives the crew energy, and the level path opens more: level 3 the triple merge (a level 3 or bigger part dropped on its twin while a third touches it makes two), level 5 Tide tokens from some crew orders, level 8 crew favours (a harder part for a Sea chest, or a Brass cog at the Survey Worksite). Tap the level badge to see it.
    10. The shed under the board keeps four parts, crates, cogs or tokens off the board: drag one onto a shed space (or tap it and choose Shed). Tap a shed space to bring it back. Things in the shed do not count for orders.
    11. The collection book (📖 Book, at the bottom) has a page for each generator's chain. Every part you make for the first time waits there: tick it off for a few coins (more for higher tiers). Find and tick a whole generator page for a Brass cog.
    12. Rare extras: the Rivet Rover and the Survey Kite sometimes make a rare extra part as well as their own, at no extra energy (Bolts, Note fragments). One comes at the latest on the 14th tap without one; while three lie unused and no order wants one, they rest (those taps do not count). Merge them up like any part; some crew orders ask for the extras you hold, others for one more to come, and pay more for them. Their own book pages fill as you find them.
    13. The shop (🛒 Shop) sells energy, crates, a Tide token and a Brass cog for coins, a few of each a day; coins spent there are not spent on the coast. Two offers are free if you watch a short ad: energy, and a driftwood crate.
    14. A daily gift waits each new day: energy, coins, a crate, a Tide token, and a Brass cog on the seventh. A missed day does not reset it.
    15. Crates give free parts: tap twice. Two unopened twin crates make a bigger one (driftwood crate → sea chest → captain's trunk).
