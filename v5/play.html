Coast Camp

A new way to play Vaultwake: crew orders earn coins, coins upgrade the coast.

Play Classic Vaultwake

How to play

  1. Tap a generator for a part (1 ϟ). Tap a crate twice for a free part.
  2. Tap a part, then its twin to merge them.
  3. Deliver crew orders for coins.
  4. Go to the beach (BEACH button) and spend coins there: each place is upgraded in three stages, and each stage changes the beach and tells a bit of the story.
  5. Sealed cargo surrounds Rivet Rover's camp in the middle of the board. Each crate shows the well-merged part that opens it (deeper crates want higher parts): lay that part on it. Inside: parts, crates, coins, a little energy and Tide tokens. The more useful the find, the harder the part it asks for; tokens glow green. A Tide token laid on a part turns it into a part of the same tier that a crew order wants.
  6. Tideglass half-buries parts around the camp. It takes no part: a merge beside it cracks it if what it makes is at least as strong as the part inside; the third crack breaks it and the part is yours.
  7. The tide covers the outer ring of the board. Each side dries when a place on the beach is built: the picture on the water shows which one (the boardwalk, the signal mast, the supply pier, the beacon).
  8. Level up a generator to find the next one: at level 3, Rivet Rover brings out the Survey Kite; the Kite brings out the Rope Winch, then the Shell Trap and the Lantern Kiln. Generators grow by merging: drag a generator onto another of the same kind and level (Rivet Rover Lv1 + Lv1 → Lv2). Higher levels make better parts. Copies come from upgrading the beach (the boardwalk, the Workshop, the signal flags and later stages) and from level chests.
  9. Crates give free parts: tap twice. Two unopened twin crates make a bigger one (driftwood crate → sea chest → captain's trunk).
