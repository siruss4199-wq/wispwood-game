COAST CAMP

Coast Camp

A new way to play Vaultwake: crew orders earn stars, stars restore the coast.

Play Classic Vaultwake

How to play

  1. Tap a generator for a part (1 ϟ). Tap a crate twice for a free part.
  2. Tap a part, then its twin to merge them.
  3. Deliver crew orders for ★ stars.
  4. Spend stars on the map to restore the coast, one place at a time.
  5. Sealed cargo covers the board. Each crate shows the part that opens it: lay that part on it. Inside: parts, crates, energy, stars — and new generators.
  6. Crates give free parts: tap twice. Two unopened twin crates make a bigger one (driftwood crate → sea chest → captain's trunk). Drag a captain's trunk onto the generator of its colour to level it up.
